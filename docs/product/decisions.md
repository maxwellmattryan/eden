## How to use this doc

Cite ids; never restate. To settle an open question, add a `D-n` entry and mark the `OQ-n` "promoted to D-n". To change a decision, add a new `D-n` and mark the old one "superseded by D-n". Both stay in place.

| D-73 | The scheduler, signals and the inbox. **The tick** is the crate's, because a timer in a hidden webview is slowed or stopped: an alarm looks at the earliest schedule every thirty seconds and tells the shell, and changes nothing. A take answers each schedule once however many occurrences were missed. A schedule is `once`, `daily` at a local time or `every` so many seconds, a minute at least; a manifest declares the repeating ones (`schedules`) and a domain sets a one-shot while the app runs. `scheduler.fired` is handed to its subscribers and never stored. **A signal** is a row: a name, a payload of URIs and a few fields (4 KB at most), the tier of what it names, and an optional key under which it is emitted once. **A rule** is a notification kind of a manifest with a `signal` and an optional `when`. A card carries no words: its line is written from the rule's locale keys and the signal's payload. An OS notification needs the `os-notifications` capability on the device (D-70), which the first card that would have been one asks for, and from T2 up it names the domain and not what happened. | D-75 | The Tasks substrate, Phase 1. **Task logic lives in the frontend**, in `@eden/shared/recurrence` and `@eden/shared/tasks`, pure and tested in Node; the crate holds `recurrence`, `target` and `progress` as JSON it does not read, as it holds a fact's value (D-72). **The shapes**: a recurrence is an RRULE subset (`freq`, `start`, `interval`, `weekdays`, `until`, `count`; monthly and yearly skip a month without the day, weekly intervals count Monday weeks whatever the week start), a habit's target is a count per day or week, and progress is a record of days, each done at an instant, skipped, or tallied; what is malformed reads as `null`. **Occurrences are computed, never materialised**: a routine is one row whose `due` stays `null`, its state for a day in `progress`; a habit tallies by day, is measured over its period from the owner's week start (D-58), and its streak is recomputed and written on each tally, with up to `grace` missed periods in a row forgiven. **Snooze moves `due`**: later today (the next full hour plus two, absent once that is tomorrow), tomorrow, or the first day of next week, keeping the time of day; no column is added. **A line added on Today with no date is due today**; a task made elsewhere with no due does not appear. **Overdue** is a todo or a checklist not done whose due day, in the owner's zone, is before today; a timed task past its time today stays under Due today, marked, and a missed routine never becomes overdue. **The task signals are the frontend's** (amends the last sentence of D-73 for `task.created` and `task.completed` only): whatever creates or completes a task for the owner emits through `@eden/shared/tasks` after its write lands, one signal per owner action, the payload the URI, the kind, the day and the title clipped to 200 characters, keyed by the id for a creation and by the id and the day for a completion (the first day of the period when a habit meets its target). A batch (the seed) and an import emit nothing; a failed emit never fails the write; an undo retracts nothing.
